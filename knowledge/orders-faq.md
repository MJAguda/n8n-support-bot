# Orders FAQ

## Can I change or cancel my order?

You can change or cancel an order within **1 hour** of placing it, using the link in your order confirmation email. After that, the order is being packed and can't be changed. You can return it once it arrives (see Returns and exchanges).

## Where is my order?

Use the tracking link in your shipping confirmation email, or ask our chat assistant with your order number and the email used for the order.

## Backorders

If an item is backordered, the product page shows the expected ship date. We ship the rest of your order right away and the backordered item when it arrives, at no extra shipping cost.

## Missing items

If your package is missing an item, check whether your order was split into several shipments (each has its own tracking email). If an item is still missing, contact us within 14 days of delivery.

## Order confirmation not received

Check your spam folder. If it's not there after an hour, the email address on the order may have a typo, so contact us with your name and the last four digits of the card used.
