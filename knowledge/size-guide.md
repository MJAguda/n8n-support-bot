# Size guide

## Jackets and tops (unisex fit)

| Size | Chest (in) | Sleeve (in) |
|---|---|---|
| XS | 33-35 | 31.5 |
| S | 35-38 | 32.5 |
| M | 38-41 | 33.5 |
| L | 41-44 | 34.5 |
| XL | 44-47 | 35.5 |
| XXL | 47-50 | 36.5 |

If you are between sizes or plan to layer a fleece underneath, size up.

## Footwear

Our Cascade and Switchback hiking boots run **half a size small**. We recommend ordering half a size up from your usual sneaker size. Trail runners fit true to size. Wide (2E) widths are available for the Cascade boot.

## Gloves

Measure around your palm just below the knuckles: S is 7 to 8 inches, M is 8 to 9 inches, L is 9 to 10 inches, XL is 10 to 11 inches.

## Sleeping bags

Regular bags fit people up to 6 ft (183 cm). Long bags fit up to 6 ft 6 in (198 cm).
