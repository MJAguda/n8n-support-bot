# Payments, prices and promotions

## Payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Tide & Trail gift cards. Shop Pay Installments lets you split orders of $50 to $1,500 into 4 interest-free payments.

## Price adjustments

If an item you bought drops in price on our website within **14 days** of your order, we refund the difference once per item. Contact customer care with your order number. Price adjustments don't apply to clearance, flash sales or competitors' prices.

## Promo codes

Only one promo code can be used per order. Promo codes can't be applied to gift cards or to orders that have already been placed.

## Trail Club rewards

Trail Club members earn 1 point per $1 spent. Every 200 points becomes a $10 reward. Members get free standard shipping on every order with no minimum. Joining is free.

## Gift cards

Gift cards never expire and can be used online only.
