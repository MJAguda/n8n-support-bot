# Product care

## Down jackets and sleeping bags

Wash in a front-loading machine on a gentle cycle with a down-specific wash, in cold or warm water. Never use a top-loader with an agitator, fabric softener or dry cleaning. Tumble dry on low heat with two or three clean tennis balls until completely dry, which can take two to three hours. Store sleeping bags loose in the large cotton storage sack, not compressed in the stuff sack.

## Waterproof shells

Wash with a technical wash, then tumble dry on low for 20 minutes to restore the water-repellent finish. If water no longer beads up on the fabric, apply a spray-on DWR treatment.

## Leather boots

Brush off dirt after each hike and let boots air dry away from direct heat. Apply leather conditioner every few months. Never dry boots next to a fire or heater, as heat cracks leather and weakens glue.

## Tents

Always dry your tent completely before storing it, to prevent mildew. Clean with lukewarm water and a sponge, never in a washing machine. Store loosely in a cool, dry place.
