# Warranty

## Trail Promise

All Tide & Trail branded gear is covered by our **Trail Promise lifetime warranty** against defects in materials and workmanship, for the life of the product.

The warranty does **not** cover normal wear and tear, damage from accidents or misuse, or damage from improper care (for example, drying boots next to a fire).

Other brands we sell are covered by their manufacturer's warranty. We'll help you contact them.

## How to make a warranty claim

Email warranty@tideandtrail.example with your order number (if you have it), photos of the defect and a short description. Our team replies within 2 business days. We will repair, replace or refund the item at our discretion. If you don't have your order number, a claim can still be made with proof of purchase or by registering the product.

## Repairs

We offer paid repairs for damage the warranty doesn't cover, such as torn fabric or broken zippers from use. Repair quotes are free.
