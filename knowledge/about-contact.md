# About us and contact

Tide & Trail Outfitters is an online outdoor gear store based in Bend, Oregon, selling tents, sleeping bags, outerwear, footwear and camp gear since 2014.

## Customer care

- Chat: our assistant on the website, any time
- Email: care@tideandtrail.example (we reply within 1 business day)
- Phone: (541) 555-0199, Monday to Friday, 7 AM to 5 PM Pacific time

## Store and pickup

We are online only. There is no retail store and no in-person pickup at the warehouse.

## Sustainability

All of our down is certified to the Responsible Down Standard, and our packaging is made from recycled cardboard.
