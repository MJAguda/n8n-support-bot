# Shipping

Tide & Trail Outfitters ships from our warehouse in Bend, Oregon.

## Shipping options and costs (contiguous US)

| Option | Delivery time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 3 to 6 business days | $6.95, free on orders of $75 or more |
| Expedited | 2 business days | $14.95 |
| Overnight | Next business day | $29.95 |

- Orders placed before 1 PM Pacific time on a business day ship the same day. Orders placed later, or on weekends and holidays, ship the next business day.
- Alaska and Hawaii: Standard shipping only, 6 to 10 business days, $19.95. Free shipping does not apply.
- We ship to PO boxes and APO/FPO addresses with Standard shipping only.
- International shipping: we currently ship only within the United States.

## Tracking

You get a shipping confirmation email with a tracking link as soon as your order leaves the warehouse. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan.

## Lost or damaged packages

If tracking shows delivered but you can't find the package, check with neighbours and wait 48 hours, as carriers sometimes mark packages delivered early. Then contact us and we will open a claim with the carrier and send a replacement or refund. Report damaged items within 7 days of delivery with a photo of the item and the box.
