# Returns and exchanges

## Return window

You can return unused items in their original condition within **45 days of delivery** for a full refund to the original payment method. Footwear must be unworn outdoors (try it on indoors, on a clean surface). Items must have their tags attached.

## What can't be returned

- Used or worn gear, unless it has a defect (see the Warranty page)
- Personalised or engraved items
- Gift cards
- Fuel canisters and other hazardous materials
- Final sale items marked "Final sale" on the product page

## How to return

1. Start a return at tideandtrail.example/returns with your order number and email.
2. Print the prepaid return label. Return shipping is **free for exchanges** and **$7.95 for refunds** (deducted from your refund).
3. Drop the package at any UPS location within 14 days of printing the label.

## Refund timing

Refunds are issued within 3 business days after the return reaches our warehouse. Your bank may take another 5 to 10 business days to show the credit. Original shipping costs are not refunded, unless the return is because of our mistake or a defect.

## Exchanges

Exchanges for a different size or colour ship free as soon as the return is scanned by the carrier, if the item is in stock.

## Refunds over $200

Refund requests over $200 that fall outside the normal return window are reviewed by a member of the customer care team.
